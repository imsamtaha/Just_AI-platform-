# JUST AI Marketing

## Workflow
1. Define audience, problem, offer, positioning, and objective.
2. Build the message and proof points.
3. Select channels and formats.
4. Create campaign assets and calls to action.
5. Define metrics and experiments.

## Rules
- Lead with customer value.
- Keep claims accurate and supportable.
- Prefer testable hypotheses over assumptions.

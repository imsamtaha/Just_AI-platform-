# JUST AI Business Strategy

## Workflow
1. Define the business objective and constraints.
2. Identify customer, problem, value proposition, and economics.
3. Analyze competition and differentiation.
4. Generate options and evaluate impact, feasibility, risk, cost, and speed.
5. Recommend a path with measurable milestones and KPIs.

## Rules
- Distinguish assumptions from facts.
- Surface trade-offs.
- Connect every recommendation to an action or decision.

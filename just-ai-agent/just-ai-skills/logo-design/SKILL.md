# JUST AI Logo Design

## Workflow
1. Define brand purpose, audience, personality, and positioning.
2. Develop distinct conceptual directions.
3. Evaluate silhouette, geometry, typography, negative space, and scalability.
4. Define color and monochrome variants.
5. Specify clear-space, minimum size, background, and misuse rules.

## Rules
- Design for recognition and simplicity.
- Ensure the mark works in monochrome.
- Do not copy protected logos or create confusingly similar marks.

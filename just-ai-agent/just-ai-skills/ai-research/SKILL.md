# JUST AI Research

## Workflow
1. Define the research question and decision it supports.
2. Break it into factual sub-questions.
3. Prefer primary sources and official documentation.
4. Verify time-sensitive claims.
5. Separate facts, inference, and uncertainty.
6. Synthesize findings into actionable conclusions.

## Rules
- Never invent citations.
- Prefer primary sources.
- State material uncertainty and evidence gaps.

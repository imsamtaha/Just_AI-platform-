# JUST AI Sales

## Workflow
1. Define ICP and objective.
2. Research the prospect using verified information.
3. Identify likely business problems and value.
4. Qualify fit, need, authority, timing, and feasibility.
5. Prepare concise personalized communication.
6. Advance to a clear next step and update pipeline state.

## Rules
- Never invent facts about a prospect.
- Avoid deceptive urgency or pressure.
- Protect personal and confidential information.
